01 Featured · Simulation

BEVNav: camera-only indoor navigation

A ROS 2 navigation stack for a ground rover with one RGB camera on a pan joint and no range sensor. Each camera image becomes a bird's-eye-view occupancy grid that the rover plans and drives on, and keeps as a map of the space it has explored.

  • Six-heading pan scan fused in log-odds into a 360° grid; every view feeds a persistent 50 × 50 m map.
  • Cost map, greedy planner and 20 Hz pure-pursuit controller, with shortest-route sub-goals so the rover can back out of dead ends.
  • Optional SegFormer-B0 floor segmentation, fine-tuned on data collected in simulation.

03 Real robot · Raspberry Pi

Active perception for a mobile robot

A rover that judges the quality of its own camera view, works out why it is poor, and moves to fix it.

  1. Camera
  2. YOLOv8
  3. View score
  4. Corrective move
  5. Re-score
  • Scores sharpness, lighting, framing and occlusion with noise-, exposure- and scale-robust OpenCV metrics, and flags the weakest factor.
  • Closed-loop decision layer picks a corrective move and re-scores; after a fixed number of attempts it returns to the best view seen.
  • Calls a vision-language model for occlusion and framing only after a fast local check passes.

04 ROS 2 Humble

Stateful ROS 2 perception system

Gives an object detector a memory: a persistent, queryable record of what the robot saw and where.

  1. Camera
  2. YOLOv8 + ByteTrack
  3. SQLite memory
